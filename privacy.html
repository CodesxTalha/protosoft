Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and who can see it.

Last updated 10 November 2026

TL;DR

We collect only what you type into the booking form, plus your country to show the right currency. We don’t sell your data, and we don’t use ads or tracking.

What you give us

When you book a call, you give us:

  • your name and email address,
  • your phone number and profession, if you choose to add them,
  • the services and budget you pick, and any note you write,
  • the date and time you choose for the call.

We use this to book the call, to contact you about it, and to prepare for it. We don’t use it for anything else, and we don’t sell it or share it for marketing.

Your country

To show prices in the right currency (rupees for Pakistan, dollars for everywhere else), the site looks up your country from your IP address. Your browser sends a request to a public lookup service, which sees your IP address as any website you visit does. The result is a country code. We don’t save it, and we don’t store your IP address.

Who handles your information

We use trusted third-party services to run the site and the booking: for the calendar, the video call link, email, and hosting. Your details are handled by them only to provide those services, under their own terms and privacy policies. The site also loads fonts and scripts from outside services, which can see your IP address when a page loads, as with most websites.

We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and the site doesn’t store anything in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep booking details for as long as we need them to work with you, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back to us. Ask us to delete them at any time and we will.

Your choices

You can ask to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Tell us through the booking page or by replying to any email we have sent you.

Children

This site is for businesses and adults. If you are under 18, please don’t send us your details.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date at the top changes with it.
