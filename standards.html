Our standards

The work we take, the work we don’t, and the lines we keep.

Last updated 10 November 2026

TL;DR

We don’t build for gambling or anything illegal. We generally keep images of women out of our websites, with exceptions we decide case by case.

Work we don’t take

We say no to these projects, whatever the budget:

  • Gambling. Betting, casinos, lotteries, and anything that works like them.
  • Anything illegal. Illegal goods or services, or a business that breaks the law where it operates.
  • Scams and misleading sites. Fake reviews, false claims, get-rich-quick schemes, or sites made to trick people.
  • Adult content.

If we find out after we start that a project falls into one of these, we can stop. The refund section of our refund policy applies to anything not yet worked on.

Images of people

By default we don’t put photographs of women in the websites we build. This is simply how we work. It says nothing against anyone, and we value the work of women as much as anyone’s. We build with product shots, objects, type, colour and illustration instead.

Some projects do call for it, such as a women’s business, a team page or a personal portfolio. In those cases we can work with images that are modest, respectful and honest about the person or the product. We don’t use images meant to attract attention for the wrong reasons.

If you have a project like that, tell us before we start and we will agree what works.

How we work

  • We tell you plainly what we can and can’t do, and what it costs, before work starts.
  • We treat what you share with us as private. See our privacy policy.
  • We don’t make up numbers, reviews or results for you or for ourselves.
