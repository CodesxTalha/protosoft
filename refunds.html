Refund policy

When you can get your money back, and when you can’t.

Last updated 10 November 2026

TL;DR

Not started: you get your money back. Started or finished: no refund.

Before we have started

If we haven’t started work on your project, you can cancel and we’ll refund what you’ve paid in full. If a payment provider charged a fee to take or return your money, that fee comes out of the refund.

Work has started once we have begun designing, writing, building or setting anything up for you, including the first draft and any call spent on your brief after payment.

After we have started

Once work has started, what you have paid is not refundable. Our time goes in from the first day, so the payment covers work already done and the time we set aside for you. You can stop the project at any point, and we will hand over what has been finished and paid for.

After we have delivered

When the work is complete and delivered, it is not refundable. Before that point we will fix what doesn’t match what we agreed.

If we can’t finish

If we stop or can’t complete the project for a reason that is ours, you get back the part of your payment that covers the work we haven’t done. The same goes if we end a project under our standards.

Costs we paid for you

Domains, hosting, licences and other things we bought on your behalf are not refundable once they are bought. They stay yours.

How to ask

Tell us through the booking page or by replying to any email we have sent you. We reply to refund requests as soon as we can, and send approved refunds back the way you paid.
