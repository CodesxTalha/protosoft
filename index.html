How a project runs.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery

    We start with a call about your business, your customers and what the project needs to do. Then we agree on scope, timeline and budget.

  2. Step 02

    Planning

    We write down what will be built: pages and features, content, milestones and dates. Everyone agrees on the plan before design starts.

  3. Step 03

    Design

    We design the brand, layout and key screens first and share them for feedback. The direction is settled before any code is written.

  4. Step 04

    Development

    We build the site or app, share previews along the way and test it on real devices.

  5. Step 05

    Launch

    Final checks, domain and hosting setup, then we go live. We walk your team through how to manage it.

  6. Step 06

    Support

    After launch we fix bugs and make updates. When you are ready, we can add marketing or automation.
