How we work.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery

    We start with a call to understand your business, audience and goals, then agree on scope, timeline and budget.

  2. Step 02

    Planning

    We turn what we learned into a clear plan: pages and features, content structure, timeline and milestones, so everyone agrees before design begins.

  3. Step 03

    Design

    Brand, layout and key screens are designed first and shared for feedback, so the direction is settled before any code is written.

  4. Step 04

    Development

    We build your website or app with clean, fast code, share regular previews, and test it on real devices.

  5. Step 05

    Launch

    Final checks, domain and hosting setup, and a smooth go-live, with a walkthrough so your team can manage it.

  6. Step 06

    Support

    After launch we stay close with fixes, updates and improvements, plus marketing and automation when you are ready to grow.
